          Gesture check

          Hold up a gesture and check which fingers are detected. If a gesture is misread, try the tips below.

          Thumb counts easilyThumb must stick out
          • 4 fingers shows as Open hand? Move the slider right, or tuck your thumb into your palm.
          • Open hand shows as 4 fingers? Move the slider left and spread your thumb wide.
          • Gestures flicker? Set gesture sensitivity towards Steady and use even light from the front.
          • Hands swapped? Turn Mirror preview on, or switch Left-handed.

          How to play

          1. Left hand picks the chord. Show 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 fingers, the rock sign or the shaka. Close your fist to stop.
          2. Right hand shapes the sound. Raise it to get louder, lower it to fade. Finger shapes change the chord's colour.
          3. Sing along. Chords keep ringing as long as you hold the shape. Two fists stop everything.
